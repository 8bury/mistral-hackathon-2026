## Slide 5 · The product
"Using it takes three steps.
**Point** the camera, or pick a photo from your gallery.
**Ask**: hold the mic and just say it.
**See and hear**: the spot lights up and your guide answers out loud."

## Slide 6 · The magic moment
"This is the moment we built everything around.
The frame freezes, so you and the guide are looking at the same picture.
A box and a pulsing point mark exactly what the answer is about.
The guide speaks while the caption appears word by word.
And three follow-up questions, written for *this* answer, let you dig deeper with one tap."

*Slow down on this slide; it's the core of the pitch.*

## Slide 7 · The walk
"Every question becomes a stop on your walk.
The nearest landmark is always on screen, and the map follows your real GPS.
At the end you get a recap (stats, a timeline, your guide's summary) to share.
That's what you take home."
