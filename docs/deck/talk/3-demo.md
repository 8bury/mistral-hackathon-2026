# Demo person: Live demo (slide 8, 60–90 s)

You only run the demo. Speaker 2 hands over to you, and Speaker 2 moves the slides on while you demo.
**Order:** Speaker 1 → Speaker 2 → **Demo** → Speaker 3.

---

## Before going on stage
- App open (phone over HTTPS, or laptop), already past the welcome screen.
- A photo of the **dragon boundary marker** ready (on screen, printed, or in the phone's gallery).
- Sound **on** and volume up. Test once before the session.
- Backup ready: offline mock build (`VITE_MOCK_API=true`) and a screen recording.

## Script (narrate each step as you do it)
1. "I'll pick a guide and switch to **Español**."
2. "I point at the dragon, hold the mic and ask: *'¿Qué es ese dragón?'*" Then **release**.
3. "The frame freezes, the dragon is highlighted, and listen…"
   **Let the answer play. Don't talk over it.**
4. "Now I'll tap a follow-up." Tap one chip. "No second photo needed."
5. "And the recap: my walk, ready to share." Open Recap from the bottom row.

*Optional if time allows:* switch to the **You** guide, type a style ("explain it like a football commentator"), and ask again.

## If something breaks
- No camera, or the angle is bad: "Let me use a photo instead." Use the **gallery** button.
- Network is slow or down: switch to the **mock** build, or play the **screen recording**.
- Keep narrating, and apologise at most once.

---

## Handoff
"So how does that actually work? [Speaker 3]."

## Q&A you own
- Anything about the live demo itself (what was on screen, why it paused, what a button does).
